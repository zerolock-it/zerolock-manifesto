# Zerolock Manifesto
Zerolock Manifesto on digital sovereignty, vendor lock-in, and the right to own your technology. Published at zerolock.org — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22201357

**Technology should serve those who use it. Not those who sell it.**

This repository contains the Zerolock Manifesto on digital sovereignty, 
vendor lock-in, and the right of organisations to own their technology 
infrastructure.

## Read the full manifesto

The manifesto is published at **https://zerolock.org**

You can also download the PDF directly from this repository.

## Academic reference

Published on Zenodo with DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22201357](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22201357)

## What the manifesto covers

- Why the current IT model was designed to create dependency
- The US Cloud Act and what it means for European businesses
- Why software you cannot own is not a tool but a dependency
- Why real security is built on open foundations
- Why IT infrastructure should be an asset, not a contract
- The concrete alternative that open source offers
